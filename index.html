The strategy in three moves

Azure is the primary cloud. Security is the differentiator. AWS becomes later expansion—not a competing beginner path.

Move 01 · now

Own infrastructure fundamentals

Build hands-on capability in identity, networks, endpoints, backups, monitoring, patching, and scripting.

Move 02 · 2027

Operate Azure confidently

Manage identity, governance, virtual networks, compute, storage, monitoring, and recovery—not just service names.

Move 03 · 2028

Secure & automate cloud

Combine security controls and threat visibility with repeatable infrastructure-as-code and a timely market move.
